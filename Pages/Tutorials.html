  • CEX2DEX Conversion Tutorial (CFW Only)

    • Rebug CEX 2 DEX & DEX 2 CEX
      • └ You need to be on Rebug REX or D-REX Not Lite and you need Rebug Toolbox installed └ Open it amd go to: Utilities Tab > Dump eid root key (ps3 will restart) └ After your PS3 Restarts: open Rebug Toolbox and go to: DEX/CEX > Rewrite Target ID in Flash and then Swap LV2 Kernel (ps3 will restart) └ To Convert to CEX do the same thing except you will only have to do: Rewrite Target ID in Flash this time
    • Evilnat CEX 2 DEX & DEX 2 CEX
      • └ You need to be on Evilnat PEX or D-PEX! └ Go to the network tab on the XMB/Home screen └ Go into: Custom Firmware Tools > Dump Tools > Dump Erk (ps3 may restart) └ then go into: Custom Firmware Tools > Cex2Dex Tools > Convert to (DEX or CEX) (ps3 may restart) └ then: Custom Firmware Tools > Cex2Dex Tools > Swap Kernel (ps3 may restart)
