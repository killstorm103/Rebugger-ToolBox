Rebugger - ToolBox: Tutorials

  • CEX2DEX Conversion Tutorial (CFW Only)

    • └ ⚠️️ I AM NOT RESPONSIBLE FOR ANY DAMAGE THIS MAY CAUSE TO YOUR PS3! ⚠️
    • Rebug CEX 2 DEX & DEX 2 CEX
      └ You need to be on Rebug REX or D-REX (not Lite) and have Rebug Toolbox installed.
      └ Open it and go to: Utilities Tab > Dump eid root key (PS3 will restart).
      └ After your PS3 restarts, open Rebug Toolbox and go to: DEX/CEX > Rewrite Target ID in Flash, then Swap LV2 Kernel (PS3 will restart).
      └ To convert back to CEX, do the same thing except you will only have to do: Rewrite Target ID in Flash this time.
    • Evilnat CEX 2 DEX & DEX 2 CEX
      └ You need to be on Evilnat PEX or D-PEX.
      └ Go to the Network tab on the XMB/Home screen.
      └ Go into: Custom Firmware Tools > Dump Tools > Dump Erk (PS3 may restart).
      └ Then go into: Custom Firmware Tools > Cex2Dex Tools > Convert to (DEX or CEX) (PS3 may restart).
      └ Then: Custom Firmware Tools > Cex2Dex Tools > Swap Kernel (PS3 may restart).
