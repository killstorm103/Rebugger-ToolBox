To find your console's exact factory minimum firmware version, use this reliable manual method:

1. Download MINVERCHK.PUP.
2. On a FAT32 USB drive, create the folders PS3/UPDATE/ and place the file inside, renaming it to PS3UPDAT.PUP.
3. Insert the USB into your PS3 and go to Settings → System Update → Update via Storage Media.
4. The update will fail and show a message like: "Update data of version 3.40 or later can be installed on this system."

The number shown (e.g. 3.40) is your console's factory minimum firmware!
